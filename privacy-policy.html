Privacy Policy

Atman · Last updated: July 24, 2026

Atman ("we," "us," or "the app") is operated by Eyob Tsegaye Yosef, based in Buffalo, New York, United States. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Atman, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and authentication credentials, used to create and secure your account.
  • Financial information: when you connect a bank account through Plaid, we receive account balances, account names, and transaction history. We never receive or store your bank login credentials — those are handled directly by Plaid.
  • Content you enter: manual transactions, budgets, goals, and messages you send to the in-app coach.
  • Technical information: basic device and app-usage data necessary to operate the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your accounts, balances, spending, subscriptions, budgets, goals, and net worth.
  • To provide personalized insights and answers through the in-app coach.
  • To secure your account and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal or financial data. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not ask about or collect immigration status.

3. Third-Party Services (Data Processors)

We share data only with the service providers required to run the app:

  • Plaid Inc. — securely connects your bank accounts and provides balance and transaction data. See Plaid's privacy policy at plaid.com/legal.
  • Supabase — provides authentication and encrypted database storage for your account and financial data.
  • OpenAI — powers the in-app coach. When you use the coach, aggregated, summarized financial context (never your bank credentials or access tokens) is sent to generate a response.

Each provider processes data only to deliver its service to us and is bound by its own privacy and security obligations.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256), row-level access controls that prevent any user from accessing another user's data, and an optional Face ID / passcode lock within the app. Your bank access tokens are stored server-side and are never exposed to the app on your device.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account & All Data. This removes your profile, connected bank items, accounts, transactions, and coach messages, and revokes your bank connections through Plaid. Deletion is permanent.

6. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your data at any time through the app. To make a request or ask a question, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

Atman is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
